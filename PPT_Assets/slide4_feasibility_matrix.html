⚠️ KEY CHALLENGES & RIGOROUS MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Evaluator Defense Framework
1. Edge AI / Vision Failure in Glare & Dust Technical Risk

Mitigation: Sensor fusion fallback. If optical camera confidence drops below 60%, deterministic Ultrasonic (SEN1) and Laser ToF (SEN2) automatically take over safety haptics.

2. OS Freeze / Raspberry Pi Kernel Panic Reliability Risk

Mitigation: Asymmetric dual-core architecture. The ESP32-S3 runs independently on FreeRTOS. Obstacle alerts, drop-off detection, and LTE SOS continue with Pi dead.

3. Mechanical Tip Motor Stall on Stones Mechanical Risk

Mitigation: TI DRV8830 overcurrent stall detection auto-cuts motor power within 15ms. Dual mechanical return springs automatically retract the linkages safely.

4. Cellular 4G Dead-Zones in Rural Areas Connectivity Risk

Mitigation: Multi-band SIM7600G-H module falls back to 2G GSM SMS. In local dead zones, 92dBA internal siren sounds and GPS coordinates buffer to flash memory.
