📋 Primary User Survey Insights

N = 72 PwDs

Conducted in partnership with Bangalore blind schools & National Association for the Blind (NAB).

1. Head-Level Hanging Obstacles 92% Suffer Injuries
Low tree branches, shop awnings & open truck beds completely missed by traditional cane.
2. Silent Electric Vehicles & Scooters 86% Report Fear
EVs lack engine acoustic rumble; users unable to detect approaching vehicles in quiet streets.
3. Earphones Blocking Traffic Sounds 89% Reject Earbuds
Existing smart canes require phone earphones that plug the ear canal—considered a death trap.
4. Sudden Puddles & Muddy Slipping 79% Experience Slips
Monsoon potholes & standing dirty water cannot be sensed before shoes step in.
5. Phone App Crashes & Bluetooth Lag 74% Discard Apps
Smartphones overheat, disconnect BLE, or drain battery within 4 hours outdoors.
