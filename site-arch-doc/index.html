§ 1 — System Overview
7 System Architecture Domains
Dual-processor layered architecture: ESP32-S3 hard real-time safety controller + Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W edge AI gateway.
🔵
1. Perception & Sensor Array
Multi-Modal Sensing

Dual-tier optical, ultrasonic, thermal, and spatial environmental perception with 360° coverage from head height to pavement level. 9 sensor nodes across 5 modalities.

🔴
2. Real-Time Safety (RTOS)
Always-On Hard Safety Path

ESP32-S3 running deterministic 50ms FreeRTOS safety loops. Operates completely autonomously even if Pi, cellular, or cloud are offline. Hardware watchdog in <10ms.

🟣
3. Edge AI & High-Level Gateway
Computer Vision & Voice NLP

Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W hosting dual-camera vision models (YOLO-Fastest / MobileNet), Whisper.tflite speech recognition, and Gemini multimodal cloud synchronizer.

🟢
4. Audio Assistant & Tactile HMI
Assistive Ear Link & Haptics

Hands-free bone-conduction Bluetooth ear assistant, top apex voice microphone, vibrotactile haptic palm cues (DRV2605L), and 4 tactile Braille buttons with SOS.

🟡
5. Physical & Mechanical Architecture
Quad-Pod Adaptive Tip

Ergonomic thumb-arch handle, modular split-housing electronics channel, shock-absorbing shaft, and motor-deployed quad-pod adaptive stabilization tip (DRV8830).

🔵
6. Power & Energy Distribution
5 Switched Rails + 1S2P

6800 mAh Li-Ion 1S2P battery pack feeding 5 isolated switched voltage rails: 3V3_MAIN, 5V_PI, 5V_AUX, VBAT_MODEM, VBAT_MOTOR with 4-stage power-save state machine.

🟣
7. Wireless, SOS & Caregiver Cloud
Dual-Path Emergency Link

SIM7600G-H LTE Cat-4 modem with hardware GNSS/GPS. Dual-link: ESP32-S3 UART for fail-safe SOS + RPi USB for cloud telemetry and Gemini API queries.

§ 3 — Power Rail Definitions
5 Isolated Switched Voltage Rails
Battery: 2× Panasonic NCR18650GA in 1S2P parallel configuration. Nominal: 3.6V, max: 4.2V, cut-off: 2.8V. Total capacity: 6800 mAh (24.5 Wh).

3V3_MAIN

Always-On Safety Rail
TPS63020 Buck-Boost (96% eff). Powers ESP32-S3, IMU, ToF, PIRs, Haptic IC, Moisture Sensor, PPG sensor, and all safety-critical logic. Regulated from 3.0V–4.2V cell with no interruption.

Regulator: TPS63020 · 2.4MHz · Up to 2.0A · Always-On

5V_PI

Switched Raspberry Pi Rail
TPS61088 Boost → TPS22918 switch. Powers RPi Zero 2 W, CAM1 (OV5647, 250mA), CAM2 (CMOS, 190mA). Cut by TCA9555 when battery <20% to extend runtime to 36+ hours.

Regulator: TPS61088 · 1.0MHz · 3.5A max · Switchable

5V_AUX

Switched Peripheral Rail
TPS61088 Boost (shared converter, separate switch). Powers JSN-SR04T ultrasonic (15mA), MAX98357A audio amp (600mA peak). Stays ON in Power-Save so ultrasonic safety still works.

Regulator: TPS61088 (shared) · Independent TPS22918 gate

VBAT_MODEM

Direct Cell Modem Rail
Fused direct connection from battery with 1000µF bulk capacitor to absorb 2A RF bursts. ESP32-S3 controls PWRKEY. Activated for SOS dispatch or cloud telemetry sync only.

No regulator (direct VSYS) · 2A peak RF · 1000µF buffer cap

VBAT_MOTOR

Fused Actuator Rail
Fused direct 3.7V battery rail via resettable PTC fuse (500mA hold). Powers DRV8830 tip motor driver for 1.2s deployment pulses. Hardware current limit prevents stall damage.

Fused VSYS · PTC 500mA resettable · DRV8830 H-bridge
